# Gaintropy Landing Page — Design Brainstorm

## Context
Gaintropy is a prescriptive industrial AI company. Brand colors: Obsidian Black (#0A0A0A), Forge Orange (#FF5A00), Titanium Silver (#E0E0E0), Algorithmic Cyan (#00E5FF). The confirmed logomark is a G where white scattered dots (entropy) flow clockwise and converge into a solid orange G body, with a glowing orange Pareto point dot at the inner corner.

Target buyer: VP of Operations, Chief Manufacturing Officer, Plant Manager at mid-market industrial manufacturers.

<text>
**Approach 1: Industrial Dark Room**
Design Movement: Bauhaus meets Control Room Brutalism
Core Principles: (1) Dark-first, data-forward — everything serves the data; (2) Asymmetric tension — left-heavy layouts with right-side data panels; (3) Monochromatic base with single-color punctuation; (4) Type as structure — headlines ARE the layout.
Color Philosophy: Pure obsidian backgrounds. The forge orange is used ONLY for moments of decision and action — CTAs, key numbers, the Pareto point. Everything else is white or silver. The restraint makes the orange feel like an alarm going off.
Layout Paradigm: Horizontal split-screen sections. Left: large typographic statement. Right: live data visualization or product screenshot. No centered hero — the page feels like a control room dashboard, not a marketing site.
Signature Elements: (1) Thin horizontal rule lines dividing sections, like instrument panel separators; (2) Monospace numbers for statistics — $4,200/hr, 88%, 1.2 seconds; (3) The G logomark particle animation as a subtle background element.
Interaction Philosophy: Hover states reveal data. Scroll triggers the Observe→Predict→Prescribe animation. The page teaches the product as you scroll.
Animation: Scroll-driven. As user scrolls into the "Predict" section, sensor numbers start flickering red. As they scroll into "Prescribe," ranked options slide in with savings numbers counting up.
Typography System: Display: Space Grotesk Bold (wide-tracked, all-caps for section headers). Body: Inter Regular. Data/numbers: JetBrains Mono. Hierarchy: 72px hero → 48px section → 24px subhead → 16px body.
</text>

**Approach 2: Scientific Instrument**
Design Movement: Swiss International Typographic Style meets Precision Engineering
Core Principles: (1) Grid as gospel — every element snaps to an 8px grid; (2) Information density without clutter; (3) The product IS the hero — no stock photography, only real UI; (4) Credibility through restraint.
Color Philosophy: Near-white background (#F5F5F5) with obsidian text. The forge orange appears only on interactive elements and key metrics. Algorithmic cyan used for data lines and charts. The palette says "this was designed by engineers, not marketers."
Layout Paradigm: Strict 12-column grid. Sections alternate between full-bleed dark (obsidian) and light (near-white). The contrast between sections creates rhythm without decoration.
Signature Elements: (1) Fine-line technical diagrams (the Observe→Predict→Prescribe flow as a circuit diagram); (2) Data tables with monospace fonts showing real scenario numbers; (3) The Pareto curve as a recurring decorative motif.
Interaction Philosophy: Minimal animation. Precision over spectacle. Hover states show tooltips with technical definitions. The site respects the buyer's intelligence.
Animation: Subtle. Numbers count up on scroll-enter. Lines draw themselves. No parallax, no particle effects — those are for consumer apps.
Typography System: Display: Monument Extended or Neue Haas Grotesk (ultra-wide, authoritative). Body: Helvetica Neue or Inter. Data: IBM Plex Mono. Extreme weight contrast: 900 weight headlines against 300 weight body.
</text>

<text>
**Approach 3: Forge — Dark Industrial Cinematic**
Design Movement: Cinematic Industrial Noir
Core Principles: (1) The page is a story, not a brochure — it takes you from chaos to order; (2) Full-bleed dark sections with dramatic lighting; (3) The product demo is embedded, not linked; (4) Every section earns the next CTA.
Color Philosophy: Deep obsidian throughout. Forge orange is used dramatically — large glowing numbers, the CTA button, the Pareto point. Algorithmic cyan appears only in the product UI screenshots. The palette feels like a steel mill at night: dark, hot, precise.
Layout Paradigm: Vertical narrative scroll. Each section is a chapter. Hero: the brand statement. Act 1: The problem (chaos, entropy, $4,200/hr burning). Act 2: The gap (traditional analytics stops here). Act 3: The solution (prescribe). Act 4: The proof (20 industries, real numbers). Act 5: The call (request access).
Signature Elements: (1) The G logomark particle animation — dots swirling and converging — as the hero background; (2) A live-ish demo panel embedded mid-page showing the Observe→Predict→Prescribe flow; (3) Industry selector grid with hover states showing scenario costs.
Interaction Philosophy: The page performs. Scroll triggers narrative beats. The "traditional analytics stops here" moment is a full-bleed dark section with a single red alert, then silence, then the orange prescribe panel slides in.
Animation: Cinematic. The hero G animates on load — particles swirling, converging, the orange dot appearing last. Scroll-driven section transitions. The prescribe options count up their savings values.
Typography System: Display: Space Grotesk Black (wide-tracked). Subheads: Space Grotesk Medium. Body: Inter Regular. Numbers/data: JetBrains Mono. The type system feels machined.

## CHOSEN APPROACH: Approach 3 — Forge (Dark Industrial Cinematic)

This is the right choice because:
1. The narrative scroll matches the product's core story arc (entropy → gain)
2. The dark palette is on-brand and differentiates from every white-background industrial SaaS competitor
3. The embedded demo concept turns the landing page into a live product experience
4. The cinematic approach matches the ambition of the company — this is not a feature comparison site, it's a statement
